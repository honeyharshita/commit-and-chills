Your AP agent isn't dumb. It's amnesiac.

It escalates the same Sunrise freight invoice for the 40th time, unaware the controller said yes 39 times.

I built Precedent to fix that, using Hindsight agent memory:

- Retain every reviewer resolution, timestamped
- Recall vendor-specific precedent before each decision
- No citation, no autonomy: no cited precedent means escalate
- Policy limits enforced in code, so memory can't outrank them

Before: every exception goes to a human.
After: known patterns resolve themselves, and when a vendor's contract changes, corrected precedent replaces the old rule.

Hindsight over a plain vector store handled updates and timing for me.
